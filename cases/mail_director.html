The inbox assistant that never sends

One line on a forwarded mail became a checked draft with the file attached, in 48 minutes, with 15 AI helpers.

The problem

A busy engineer gets more mail than he can answer well, and the hard part is not typing: it is checking the facts, the history and the attached files before replying. I wanted an assistant that prepares the reply for me, checks it, and leaves the decision and the Send button with me.

What the AI did

  1. On its scheduled run it collected 17 new mails and 23 mails I had sent that were still waiting for an answer.
  2. Two copies of the AI judged every mail separately; 37 were judged and only 1 went ahead, 2 were marked 'ask me' and 34 were left alone.
  3. Two research helpers dug into the background: my last send of the same data on 8 April 2026, and a check of the new data file against the plant records (182 of 183 days matched, one day to ask me about).
  4. A checker confirmed 54 facts against their sources, then a writer prepared the reply.
  5. A checker and a reviewer tested the draft: version 1 FAILED with 6 issues (one paragraph the file did not support), version 2 PASSED.
  6. It saved the draft and a short AI Note (6 points for me to decide) in my Outlook Drafts and sent me a WhatsApp summary.

The result

One ready draft with the data file attached unchanged, and a note of 6 points for me to decide. 48 minutes on its own, 15 AI helpers. Nothing was sent to anyone.

What the engineer still did

I wrote one line on a mail I forwarded to myself. I read the draft and the note, decide, and press Send myself.

Time taken

48 minutes for the whole run (26 October 2026, 19:45 to 20:33 Cairo).

Guardrails

It can save drafts only; it can never send mail. Every reply passes an independent checker and reviewer, and a failed version is rewritten, not shipped.

Copy it

Give the AI read access to your mailbox and a place to save drafts, never send rights. Ask for two independent judges, a fact checker and a reviewer before any draft is saved, and a short note of what you must decide.
