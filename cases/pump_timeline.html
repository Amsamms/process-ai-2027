A pump problem, from 15 mails to one page

A three-month mail thread with 25 attachments became one dated timeline and a short status mail.

The problem

One of two twin charge pumps was underperforming. The case had grown into a three-month mail thread between our process team, the Machinery department and the pump maker, with reports, curves and data files attached to different replies. My manager needed one clear picture of where it stood.

What the AI did

  1. Downloaded the exported mailbox file and unpacked all 15 mails (March to June 2026).
  2. Saved and read the 25 attachments (9 different documents): test reports, pump curves and a process data workbook with 781 rows per pump.
  3. Put 18 dated events in order and tagged each one by department: our team, Machinery, the pump maker.
  4. Put the three performance tests side by side: Pump B at 89.5 % and 88.2 % of its datasheet head, Pump A at 96.6 %.
  5. Flagged what was still open: no reply from the pump maker about 10 weeks after the case was sent, and one forwarded mail that bounced.
  6. Wrote a short status mail for my manager with the 5 key mails attached as numbered PDFs, sent to my own inbox.

The result

A one-page timeline and a status mail ready to forward. The numbers: Pump B head 89.5 % of datasheet (88.2 % on the re-test, with motor current at 115 % of expected), against 96.6 % for its twin. Most likely an internal problem in Pump B; the pump maker's answer was still awaited on 2 June 2026.

What the engineer still did

The diagnosis came from the process team's own tests and reports; the AI organised it, it did not discover it. I asked for 4 changes to the mail (less styling, departments shown clearly, Outlook-like PDFs, two attachments with broken tables) before forwarding it myself.

Time taken

About 3 minutes from the request to the timeline file, about 1 hour to the final mail (prompt and file times).

Guardrails

It sent the mail only to my own inbox. Forwarding to my manager, and any contact with the Machinery department or the pump maker, stayed with me.

Copy it

Export the thread from Outlook as a .pst file and put it where the AI can read it. Ask for a dated timeline with the department of each step, then a short status mail with the key mails attached. Check the attachments visually before you forward.
