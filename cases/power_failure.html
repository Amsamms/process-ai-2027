Checking a power failure report

A draft report, checked unit by unit against the plant's own emergency instructions before it went out.

The problem

After a site-wide power failure, each area writes a report of what happened, unit by unit. The draft for my area came to me. Before it goes further, someone has to check it is complete and that what the operators did matches the plant's emergency instructions.

What the AI did

  1. Found the report in my work mail (after I told it the right folder) and turned the Word file into text and a PDF, so it could see the trend charts too.
  2. Read only my area's section: 5 unit sections, each a timeline of events from the trip to the restart.
  3. Listed the 7 units running in my area and found that 2 of them had no section in the report.
  4. Read the 6 emergency work instructions for these units in our document library, plus the electrical failure sections of the manuals.
  5. Compared each timeline with its instruction and listed the steps that the instruction asks for but the report does not mention.
  6. Gave me a ranked list (must fix, important, nice to have) and saved its method as a checklist for the next power failure.

The result

Two running units were missing from the report. Steps the instructions ask for were not mentioned, for example bleeding a reactor down to 7 bar(g) if power is not back within one hour, and sending the product to the slop tank. The introduction still carried the date of an older report, and the emergency generator and UPS were not covered.

What the engineer still did

I pointed it to the right mail folder and told it the trends were pictures. I read the review, and I decide what goes back to the author and how. A missing line in a report does not mean the step was not done on the day.

Time taken

Under 1 hour 20 minutes from my request to the review I accepted (prompt and file times).

Guardrails

Read-only: it read the mail and the document library and wrote only in its own project folder. It did not reply to the author or send anything.

Copy it

Put your emergency work instructions and manuals in one folder the AI can read. When a report arrives, ask it to check the scope first (is every unit there?) and then each timeline against its own instruction, as a ranked list.
