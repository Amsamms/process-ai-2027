Ask the AI from any WhatsApp chat

I typed one line on my phone; 13 minutes later the PDF was in the chat, checked against the source.

The problem

Engineers often need one document fast, away from the desk. My work archive lives on a cloud machine, not on my phone. I wanted to ask from WhatsApp, in any chat, and get the file back in that same chat.

What the AI did

  1. Picked up my /bot message (only messages I send can start it) and handed the search to the second machine that holds the mail archive, with the instruction to send nothing to anyone.
  2. Searched the mail archive and 1,705 PDFs on disk, pulled 19 PDFs out of old mails, and looked at the best pages as images.
  3. Posted the heater datasheet PDF and the design composition into the chat, and said that no document mentions a brand-new heater.
  4. When I said I meant the fuel gas unit's manual, it found that unit has no fired heater and pulled the unit's fuel gas design basis instead.
  5. Read a scrambled, rotated table by rendering the page, checked that the values add up to 100.08 mol %, and matched them on 3 pages of the full 95 MB manual.
  6. Posted the right PDF into the chat with 13 components in mol %, pressure, temperature and heating value.

The result

The first answer came 13 minutes after my message. The corrected answer, the fuel gas design basis I actually wanted, came 8 minutes after my correction, checked against the source on 3 pages.

What the engineer still did

I had to say which document I meant: the first answer came from a different document. The bot also said it read only the main fuel gas case and did not check the second case against the page image, so that check is mine.

Time taken

13 minutes for the first answer, 8 minutes for the corrected one (run logs).

Guardrails

Only my own /bot messages start it. In shared chats it never posts passwords, account details, ID or phone numbers, and it will not message anyone else, delete data, stop services or spend money unless I confirm with /bot yes.

Copy it

Run a WhatsApp listener that watches only your own messages for a trigger word, start a headless Claude Code job with a short rules file, and post its final answer back into the same chat. Give it a way to ask the machine that holds your documents.
