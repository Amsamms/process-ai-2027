Screening a whole unit for hidden hazards

The manual, almost 3 years of plant data, the unit log and old mail: 36 places to look first, double-checked.

The problem

A known deviation from design that slowly becomes normal is a hazard in itself. I wanted the whole of one process unit searched for that pattern, not only its furnace: equipment running above a limit for long periods, alarm and trip settings that may no longer fit, and repeated events in the unit log.

What the AI did

  1. Split the 3,954-page unit manual between four helper agents (furnace, drums and blowdown, columns and utilities, alarms and trips), each writing notes with page numbers.
  2. Meanwhile read the monthly plant workbooks itself: 885 measurements as daily averages, from 2025 to September 2026, and later the 12 workbooks of 2024.
  3. Read the unit log since 2022 and searched the work-mail archive for past reports about the unit.
  4. Compared every measurement with its own limit in the manual's 865 alarm and trip settings, and looked for slow drifts that had become the normal state.
  5. Built a register of 36 hazards, each with its evidence (manual page, data, log or mail) and a screening rank from the company risk method.
  6. Had a separate agent check 95 claims from the sources: 66 confirmed, 17 partly right, 7 wrong, 5 not checkable. All corrections were applied.
  7. Rewrote the summary mail for a cold reader when I asked, and had a third agent review it cold before sending it to me.

The result

36 possible hazards, each with the evidence behind it and a first action, sent to me as one plain-English mail with 10 attachments. Two kinds of example: one compressor stage often ran above a limit in its manual (a slow drift that had become normal), and one level reading looked stuck, so it may not show a real change. Each is a question for the engineers, not a confirmed fault.

What the engineer still did

This is a screening for engineers to check, not a field finding and not a team hazard study. Every item needs a field check, and the ranks need a team to confirm them. I supplied the files and the 2024 password, and I pushed back when the first mail was not plain enough.

Time taken

About 1 hour to the verified report, about 3 hours to the final plain-English mail (session times, with my breaks).

Guardrails

Read-only on the manual, the data and the mail archive. Three independent checks (report, 2024 figures, mail); every wrong claim found was corrected before the mail. It mailed only me.

Copy it

Give the AI the unit manual, the monthly data workbooks, the unit log and the mail archive, and ask it to look for limits that are exceeded for long periods. Insist on a page or data reference for every claim, and on an independent agent checking the claims before you read the result.
